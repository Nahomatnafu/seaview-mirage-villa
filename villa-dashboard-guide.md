# Managing the villa online

A short guide to the page where you see your bookings and set your prices.

Everything here works on your phone. Save the page to your home screen and it
behaves like an app.

---

## Signing in

Go to **[your website address]/admin** and enter the password we sent you.

You stay signed in for a week, so you will not have to type it often. Nobody
else can reach this page — guests never see it, and it does not appear in
Google.

If you ever think someone else has the password, tell us and we will change it.
That signs out every device immediately.

---

## Bookings

The first screen. Every guest who pays a deposit on the website appears here on
their own, straight away — you do not have to do anything for that to happen.

For each booking you see who it is, their dates, what they have paid so far, and
what is still to come. Their email and phone number are buttons: tap to write or
call.

### Confirm or decline

A new booking arrives marked **Needs confirming**.

- **Confirm** — you are happy with it. Nothing else changes; this is you keeping
  track.
- **Decline** — you cannot take it. The dates go back on sale straight away.

**Declining does not refund anybody.** It only frees the dates on your website.
If you decline a booking, you still have to send the guest their money back in
Stripe. See the cancellation notes we sent you for how much.

### The refundable deposit

The last payment includes the $200 incidental deposit, and the booking says so.
That is the guest's money, not yours — send it back after they leave.

## Calendar

A month at a glance.

- **Black** — a guest has paid for those nights.
- **Gold** — you blocked them yourself.
- **Grey** — free.

### Blocking dates

When you take a booking over the phone or on WhatsApp, put it here. Choose the
day they arrive and the day they leave, and the website will stop offering that
week.

**Do this as soon as you take the booking.** Until it is on the calendar the
website can still sell that week to somebody else, and then you have two guests
for the same nights.

The note field is just a reminder to yourself — "booked by phone" is enough.
Please do not put guests' names or numbers there.

You can remove anything you blocked. You cannot remove a week a guest has paid
for, on purpose: that would put the villa back on sale while somebody is holding
a receipt for it.

## Rates

### Your standard rate

What a night costs on ordinary dates. Change the number and press **Save rates**
and the website updates immediately.

You can also set the **minimum number of nights**, and the **first date you are
taking bookings for**. The website will not offer anything before that date.

### Charging more at busy times

Add a period for Christmas, New Year, Easter — anything that should cost more
than usual. Give it a name, the first and last night, and the price per night.

**Each night is charged at whatever period it falls in.** If someone stays from
28 December to 4 January and your Christmas rate runs to 1 January, they pay the
Christmas rate for the first few nights and your normal rate for the rest. You
do not have to work any of that out — the website does it and shows the guest
the breakdown.

Periods cannot overlap. If you try to save two that cover the same day the page
will tell you which two, because otherwise the price for that night would be
anyone's guess.

### One thing that will not happen

**Changing your prices never changes a booking somebody has already made.**

If a guest booked a week at $2,600 a night and you later put your rate up, their
remaining payments stay exactly as agreed. This is deliberate — being billed
more than you were quoted is the fastest way to lose a guest and get a payment
reversed.

New prices apply to new bookings only.

## What still needs doing by hand

The website cannot do these, so they stay with you:

- **Refunds.** Stripe has a Refund button on each payment where you type the
  amount. The website never sends money back on its own.
- **Adding phone bookings** to the calendar.
- **Returning the $200** after a guest leaves.

---

## If something looks wrong

- **A rate change did not stick** — tell us. It usually means the storage
  connection needs attention, and it is a two-minute fix at our end.
- **A booking is missing** — check Stripe first. If the payment is there but the
  booking is not showing, tell us.
- **You cannot sign in** — we can issue a new password.

Do not worry about breaking anything on this page. The worst you can do is set a
price you did not mean to, and you can change it straight back.

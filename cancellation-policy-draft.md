## The one thing still missing

**What is the fee on standard dates?**

Your original text said **5%** in one place and **20%** in another. On a
one-week booking that's the difference between the guest losing **$910** and
losing **$3,640**.

We can't publish "a percentage" — a guest has to know the number before they
hand over $5,200, and if it isn't written down you have no basis to keep
anything if someone disputes it.

**Our recommendation: 20%.**

- It's one of the two numbers you already wrote, so it's likely what you meant.
- It sits just under your 25% deposit, so a guest who cancels never owes more
  than they've already paid. Nothing to chase.
- Cancelling 61 days out still costs you real money — staff scheduled, food
  ordered, the week hard to re-sell.

**5% is also defensible** if you'd rather compete on being flexible. It's very
generous for a villa at this price and would mean absorbing most of the loss
yourself. Your call — just pick one.

## Proposed wording

### Cancellations

Cancel on standard dates 61 or more days before arrival and your payments are
refunded, less a **[20%]** cancellation fee. Cancel 60 days or fewer before
arrival and no refund is due.

Easter and Thanksgiving bookings use a 90-day deadline. Christmas and New Year
bookings use a 120-day deadline and a **30%** fee.

No refund is given for services booked but not used, for guests who do not
arrive, or for late arrival or early departure once the rental has begun.

### Moving your dates

There is **no fee to move your dates.** We will work with you to find another
week that suits, and everything you have paid moves across to the new dates.

New dates must be within **12 months** of the original arrival date, must still
meet the 7-night minimum, and are subject to availability. If the new dates fall
in a higher-priced season, the difference is payable.

Once moved, the original dates are released and the deadlines above apply to the
new dates.

### Refunds

Refunds are returned to the card used to pay, within 5–10 business days.

**1. We did check villamimosaja.com. It has no cancellation policy on it at

We wouldn't have copied it anyway, and it's worth saying why: the policy text
you originally sent us named **"Seaview Mirage Villa Paradise"** and **"Seaview

Everything above is written for your villa, from your answers.

**2. Refund percentages cannot be set in Stripe.** You mentioned planning to set
the return percentage there. Stripe has no such setting — it is a payment
processor, not a booking system. What Stripe *does* give you is a **Refund**
button on any payment, where you type the amount you want to send back.

So the percentage has to be written in the policy, and you apply it yourself
when someone cancels. We can add a small note in your admin instructions showing
exactly what to refund for a given booking, so it's arithmetic rather than
judgement.

Leaving it blank isn't really an option: a guest who cancels will ask what they
get back, and without a published number every case becomes an argument.

**3. What is the villa's name?** You wrote "It's Seaview Mirage." The website
currently says **Sea View Mirage Villa** throughout — headings, page titles,
the logo. Do you want it changed to **Seaview Mirage**? It's a straightforward
change, but it touches every page, so we want to hear it from you rather than
guess.
